About Smart Food Rescue Network

Smart Food Rescue Network is a web application developed to reduce food wastage by connecting food donors, NGOs, and volunteers. The system ensures that excess food reaches needy people safely and efficiently.

🎯 Objectives

  • Reduce food wastage.
  • Provide food to needy people.
  • Connect donors, NGOs, and volunteers.
  • Promote social responsibility.

🌍 Mission

To create a society where no food is wasted and every needy person receives food.

🚀 Vision

To build India's most trusted food donation platform using modern technology.
